# Style Guide: New Orleans, 1996–2001

The first style pack for the merch app. Written 2026-11-08 from
`resources/ghetto_asethetic_merch/` (the New Orleans covers, contact sheet and research
notes). **Status: draft.**

---

## The look in one paragraph

Everything is too much, on purpose. A person stands in the middle of a glossy
digital collage that looks expensive: walls of fire, stacks of cash, gold, chandeliers,
luxury cars, sometimes a war zone. The light is hot and orange, everything sparkles, and the
person has a glowing halo where they were cut out and pasted in. Their name runs across the
top in huge 3D letters made of gold, chrome or diamonds, and the title sits across the
bottom. It should feel like early Photoshop and an airbrush doing their absolute most.

**Era note:** this guide covers 1996–2001, the maximalist peak. From about 2002, New
Orleans covers switch to plain photography (compare covers 20–25 in the research). That
quieter look could become a separate "minimal" pack later.

| Flame red | `#D7261E` | Fire, accent lettering, featuring lists |
| Glow yellow | `#FFE14D` | Halos around letters and people |
| Black | `#0B0B0B` | Outlines, shadows, backgrounds |
| Chrome silver | `#C9CED6` | Chrome lettering, cars |

| Money green | `#6E8B5B` | Cash stacks |

**Overall:** warm and saturated, with yellow and orange leading and black holding it
together. Cool colours only appear in chrome, diamonds and sky.

## Scenes

Each scene is a setting a design can use. A design uses one.

| Scene | What's in it | Seen in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Inferno** | Walls of flame, a burning gold palace or library, cash and jewellery flying, sparkles everywhere | *400 Degreez* |
| **Boss office** | A big desk, the person leaning back, stacks of cash, a chandelier, red velvet and gold walls, brass candlesticks, champagne | *Unlady Like* |
| **Mansion life** | Marble and gold interior, a framed gold portrait, a champagne bottle, a classic luxury car out front, a phone to the ear | *Chopper City in the Ghetto* |
| **War zone** | Explosions, jets and helicopters overhead, a tank, smoke, camo, a burning sky | *Give It 2 'Em Raw*, *Shell Shocked* |
| **Ghetto skyline** | A neighbourhood street or storefront at dusk with fire in the foreground and a dramatic sky | *Ghetto D* |
| **Crew line-up** | 3–5 people side by side, lit from below, matching headwear, fire and smoke behind | *Guerrilla Warfare*, *TRU 2 da Game* |

**Recommended first scenes:** Inferno and Boss office. They're the most recognisable and the
least likely to raise problems on merch.

## The person

**Pose and framing**

- **Hero:** waist-up or three-quarter, facing the camera, chin slightly up, centred.
- **Laid back:** seated and reclined, feet up on a desk (the Boss office scene).
- **Giant head in the sky:** a huge copy of the face fades into the top half of the scene,
  above a smaller full-body figure. This is a signature move of the era and works with any
  scene.

**Lighting:** hot, from below or behind, as if lit by fire. A bright white or yellow halo
around the edge of the person, where they were "cut out".

**Clothing options** (choices in the app, not required):

- white tee or oversized white button-up
- camo jacket or full camo
- velour or satin tracksuit
- fur coat
- bandana, du-rag or headwrap
- sunglasses

**Jewellery options:**

- gold rope chain or big gold link chain
- an iced-out pendant (a shape we design, never a real logo)
- gold fronts (grills)
- rings on several fingers, a big iced watch

Jewellery and grills change how someone looks, so they're opt-in. The face itself always
stays true to the person.

## Lettering

Drawn by the app's lettering engine, never by the AI. Six treatments cover this style:

| Treatment | How it looks | How to build it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ice** | Letters packed with sparkling diamonds or rhinestones | Fill each letter with small round gems in a grid, add a bright highlight to each gem, star glints on top, a dark outline and a white glow |
| **Gold bevel** | Thick, rounded gold letters | A vertical gold gradient, an inner bevel, a thick black outline and an outer yellow glow |
| **Chrome** | Polished metal reflecting a sky | A "horizon" gradient (light sky, dark band, bright line, brown ground), a bevel and a sharp highlight |
| **Scorched metal** | Red-hot metal letters with burn marks | A red-to-orange gradient, a black burn texture, and a yellow glow |
| **Stone** | Rough, chiselled rock letters | A rock texture, broken edges, and a white glow behind |
| **Brush script** | One word in rough white brush strokes | A brush font in white with a soft dark shadow. Used for the name only, never with another treatment on the same word |

**Supporting text:**

- **Presents line:** small serif capitals above the name, e.g. "BIG MIKE PRESENTS".
- **Featuring list:** a column of names in a heavy, narrow sans-serif, alternating red and
  white or all white, separated by commas or bullets. Long and crowded is part of the look.
- **Sticker:** our own advisory-style sticker in a box, with our own wording (for example
  "CERTIFIED HOOD CLASSIC"). Never a copy of the real Parental Advisory mark.

**Effects shared by all:** star-shaped glints on corners and edges, a glow around every
letter, and slight perspective or an arch on titles.

## Layouts

All layouts are square (1:1), like a CD cover.

| Layout | Person | Name | Title | Featuring list |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Hero** | Centre, waist-up | Huge, across the top | Across the bottom, often arched or in perspective | Right-hand column |
| **Head in the sky** | Giant face in the top half, small full-body figure below | Across the top, over the giant face | Across the bottom | Right or left column |
| **Boss desk** | Seated, reclined, centre to right | Top left, brush script | Across the bottom, large serif | Top right, under a small "featuring" heading |
| **Crew row** | 3–5 heads and shoulders in a row, centre | The crew name across the top | Across the bottom, in perspective | Small, above the title |

**Spacing rule for the AI:** leave the top fifth and bottom fifth of the picture as plain
background with no faces or important detail, so the lettering has room.

## Finish

What makes it look like 1998 instead of a modern render:

- **Oversaturated** colour with a warm orange cast
- **Hard cut-out edges** with a glowing halo around people and objects
- **Sparkles and star glints** on jewellery, letters and corners
- **Lens flares** and bright light blooms
- **An airbrushed look** on skin and surfaces: smooth, glossy, slightly unreal
- **Stacked collage depth:** objects layered in front of each other with no attempt at
  realistic perspective
- **A light print grain**, as if scanned from a CD booklet (added in code, optional)

## Prompt pieces

Ready-to-use text for the AI. A request is put together from: the **base** + one
**scene** + the **person** + the **layout space**. None of them mention an artist, album,
label or studio.

**Base (always used):**
> A square late-1990s Southern rap album cover artwork, made as a glossy digital photo
> collage with an airbrushed finish. Oversaturated warm colours, mostly gold, fire orange,
> red and black. Hard cut-out edges with a soft glowing halo around the person, sparkle
> glints and lens flares, dramatic firelight. Maximalist and luxurious, every part of the
> picture full of detail. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no stickers.

**Scenes:**

- **Inferno:** The person stands in front of a huge wall of flames inside a grand, ornate
  gold palace library that is on fire. Gold jewellery and banknotes fly through the air.
  Everything glows orange and yellow.
- **Boss office:** The person leans back in a large leather chair with their feet up on a
  polished wooden desk covered in stacks of cash. Behind them: red velvet walls with gold
  patterns, a crystal chandelier, brass candlesticks and a glass of champagne.
- **Mansion life:** The person poses in a white and gold marble mansion, holding a phone to
  their ear. Beside them, a large champagne bottle and an ornate gold picture frame. A
  classic cream-coloured luxury car with no badges or logos is parked in front.
- **War zone:** The person stands in front of a battlefield at sunset: huge orange
  explosions, smoke, fighter jets and helicopters in the sky, and a tank behind them. The
  person wears camouflage. No weapons in anyone's hands.
- **Ghetto skyline:** The person stands in front of a neighbourhood street at dusk under a
  dramatic orange and purple sky, with flames rising across the foreground.

**Person (always used, with the reference photos):**
> The person in the reference photos is the main figure, waist-up, in the centre, facing
> the viewer with a confident expression. Keep their face, skin tone, hair, facial hair and
> features exactly as in the photos, so they are clearly recognisable.
> [Clothing and jewellery choices are added here.]

**Head in the sky (optional add-on):**
> A giant version of the same person's face fills the top half of the sky, fading softly into
> the background, looking down over the scene.

**Layout space (always used):**
> Keep the top fifth and the bottom fifth of the picture as background only, with no faces
> or important objects, so lettering can be added later.

## Product notes

The cover is designed square. Each product adapts it:

| Product | How the cover fits |
| --- | --- |
| **T-shirt / hoodie** | The square cover printed large on the front, like a bootleg rap tee. Lettering can extend above or below the square. Works best on black or white shirts |
| **Poster** | The cover itself, with a border of extra fire or sky if the poster isn't square |
| **Phone case** | A tall crop: the person and the name. The title may move to the bottom. The featuring list is left out |

**Print checks:**
- thin glows and fine sparkles must still show at print size
- very dark areas can print muddy on dark shirts, so lift the shadows slightly for those

## Avoid

| Never include | Why |
| --- | --- |
| The real Parental Advisory mark | A trademark. Use our own sticker |
| Label logos, such as a gold tank emblem or a label name | Trademarks |
| Car badges and grilles that identify a brand | Trademarks. Cars stay generic |
| Designer logos on clothes or sunglasses | Trademarks |
| Real artists' faces or names, album titles | Their likeness and work |
| Realistic, readable banknotes | Copying real money has legal limits. Keep cash stylised and blurred |
| Guns in hands or pointed at the viewer | Not right for gift merch, and it can get products refused. Military vehicles and explosions in the distance are fine |
| Sexualised background figures (common on the originals) | Not right for products bought as gifts or for families |
| Anything traced or copied from a real cover | The rule for the whole project |
