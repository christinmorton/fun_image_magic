# Style Guide: Compton and LA, 1988–1996

The fourth style pack. Written 2026-11-08 from `resources/ghetto_asethetic_merch/` (the
Compton covers, contact sheet and the regional timeline). **Status: draft.** Not in the app
yet.

---

## The look in one paragraph

Sunlight and street realism. Where the South painted fantasies, LA showed the street as
it was: a crew staring down at the camera from a low angle, a lowrider on hydraulics with
gold spoke wheels, palm trees against a hazy sky, an alley, a liquor-store wall. Lettering
is loud and handmade: red brush strokes, graffiti, spray-paint stencils, and the Old English
(blackletter) type of Chicano LA. From 1992, G-funk added bright cartoon covers. Faded by
the sun, shot on film, never glossy.

**Era note:** the core is **1988–1996**. Later LA covers turn to plain photography and are
left out of this pack.

## The person

**Pose and framing**

- **Looking down:** seen from below, staring down at the camera.
- **By the car:** leaning on or crouching beside the lowrider.
- **Hero:** waist-up, arms crossed.

**Lighting:** bright, hazy daylight or golden late-afternoon sun; hard shadows.

**Clothing options:**

- a flannel shirt buttoned at the top
- a white tee
- work trousers and a plain jacket
- a plain cap or a black bandana (see Avoid)
- dark sunglasses

**Jewellery options:** a gold rope chain, a gold pendant (a shape we design). Lighter than the
South: often just one chain.

## Lettering

New treatments this pack needs:

| Treatment | How it looks | How to build it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Old English** | Blackletter capitals in black, silver or bright colours | A blackletter font, solid fill with a thin contrasting outline and a hard drop shadow |
| **Red brush** | Fast red brush strokes, slightly uneven | The brush font in red with dry-brush texture (the existing brush treatment, recoloured) |
| **Spray stencil** | Stencil letters sprayed with paint, soft overspray edges | The stencil font, a flat colour, speckled overspray around the letters, a few drips |

Also usable from New Orleans: **chrome** and **serif**.

**Font ideas** (free for commercial use, to confirm when built): *UnifrakturMaguntia* or
*Pirata One* (blackletter), *Permanent Marker* (already in the app) for brush, *Black Ops One*
(already in the app) for stencil.

## Finish

- **Sun-faded** (new finish): warm faded colours, lifted blacks, a slight yellow cast and
  35 mm film grain, as if the print had sat in a sunny window.
- **Print** (existing) also suits it.

## Prompt pieces

**Base (always used):**
> A square late-1980s or early-1990s West Coast rap album cover: a street photograph in
> bright, hazy California daylight, shot on 35 mm film, with sun-faded colours, strong blacks
> and a little film grain. Real and raw rather than glossy. No text, no letters, no numbers,
> no logos, no stickers.

**Scenes:**

- **Looking up:** Seen from the ground looking up: the person stares down into the camera
  against a pale hazy sky. No fire or flames.
- **Lowrider:** The person stands beside a classic lowrider in deep candy paint with gold
  spoke wheels on a wide palm-lined boulevard at a hazy sunset; the car is raised on its
  hydraulics. The car has no badges or logos. No fire or flames.
- **Alley:** The person stands in a sun-bleached alley with a chain-link fence, power lines
  and plain walls with no writing on them. No fire or flames.
- **Corner store:** The person stands in front of a small corner store with barred windows
  and painted shapes but no readable words, in late-afternoon sun. No fire or flames.
- **G-funk cartoon:** The whole picture is a bright, bold cartoon illustration with thick
  black outlines and flat colours: the person stands on a sunny LA street with palm trees.
  No fire or flames.
- **Crew on the block** (crews): The crew stands together on a sunny street in dark clothes,
  with a lowrider parked behind them. No fire or flames.

**Person and layout space:** the same as New Orleans (`core/styles.py`). For the G-funk
cartoon, the likeness wording should ask for the person to stay recognisable as a cartoon.

## Product notes

- **This pack prints well on white and light shirts** as well as black, thanks to its
  daylight colours.
- **Old English lettering** reads best large: keep names short or the letters get thin.

---

## Avoid

| Never include | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Gang signs, and red or blue bandanas | Gang-coded; bandanas stay black or white |
| Drug imagery: cannabis leaves, rolling papers (two covers in the research) | Not right for merch |
| Parodies of real product packaging (one cover copies a rolling-paper pack) | Trademarks |
| Sports team logos (caps and jackets are a big part of the look) | Trademarks: plain caps and jackets only |
| Car badges | Trademarks |
| Guns in hands or pointed at the viewer (the most famous cover in the research does this) | Not right for merch |
| Real artists, label names or logos | Their likeness and trademarks |
| The real Parental Advisory mark | A trademark |
| Police imagery | Not right for merch |
