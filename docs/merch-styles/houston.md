# Style Guide: Houston, 1990s–2005

The third style pack. Written 2026-11-08 from `resources/ghetto_asethetic_merch/` (the
Houston covers, contact sheet and the regional timeline). **Status: draft.** Not in the app
yet.

---

## The look in one paragraph

Slow, purple and shining. Houston's look is the **slab**: an old American luxury sedan in
candy paint so deep it looks wet, spoke rims poking far out from the wheels, the trunk
popped to show a glowing sign. Everything is bathed in purple, from giant chunky letters
to the hazy, dreamlike covers of the slowed-down "screwed" tapes. Before that, early-90s
Houston was gritty street photography: chain-link fences, prison bars, a newspaper front
page. After it, the 2000s brought chrome lettering and diamond grills in close-up.

**Era note:** the core of this pack is **1994–2005**, purple and slabs. The early-90s
street-photo look is a scene option (Front page, Behind the fence).

## The person

**Pose and framing**

- **Leaning on the slab:** waist-up or full, leaning on a candy-painted car.
- **Grill close-up:** a tight portrait with a big smile showing gold or diamond grills
  (only if grills are chosen).
- **Hero:** waist-up, centre, chin up.

**Lighting:** night light: streetlights, purple glow, chrome reflections.

**Clothing options:** a white tee, a throwback jersey (no team logos), a plain fitted cap,
a velour tracksuit.

**Jewellery options:** grills (signature for this pack), a big chain, a pendant (a shape we
design), an iced watch. Grills are opt-in, as everywhere.

## Lettering

New treatments this pack needs:

| Treatment | How it looks | How to build it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Candy** | Glossy candy-purple metal-flake letters, like car paint | The slab or block font, a deep purple gradient, fine metallic flake (bright specks), a strong glossy highlight band, a chrome outline |
| **Trunk neon** | Glowing neon script, as in a slab's trunk sign | A script font drawn as a thin bright tube with a wide coloured glow, on a dark plate |
| **Big letters** | Huge chunky letters filling the cover behind the person | The block font, candy or flat purple, cut by the person's outline so they stand in front |
| **Masthead** | A newspaper title in blackletter, with a condensed serif headline | A blackletter font for the masthead, a narrow serif for the headline, black on newsprint |

Also usable from New Orleans: **chrome** (2000s) and **ice** (grills era).

**Font ideas** (free for commercial use, to confirm when built): *Bungee* (block),
*Monoton* or *Neonderthaw* (neon), *UnifrakturMaguntia* (masthead), *Oswald* (headline).

## Finish

- **Screwed** (new finish): a purple tint, soft focus, and a faint repeated echo of the
  picture offset to one side, like the slowed, chopped tapes.
- **Print** (existing) for the slab and grill scenes.
- **Newsprint** (new, for Front page): halftone dots, off-white paper, slightly grey blacks.

## Prompt pieces

**Base (always used):**
> A square mid-1990s Houston rap album cover artwork, made as a glossy digital photo
> collage. Deep candy-paint colours, mostly candy purple with chrome, night blue and touches
> of candy red. Night lighting with streetlight glow and sharp chrome reflections. Slow,
> smooth and shining. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no stickers.

**Scenes:**

- **Slab parade:** The person stands in front of a line of classic American luxury sedans
  in deep candy paint at night, with chrome spoke rims poking far out from the wheels and
  the trunks open to show glowing lights, under streetlights. The cars have no badges or
  logos. No fire or flames.
- **Screwed haze:** The person stands in a surreal, slowed-down purple dream: soft focus,
  faint double images, floating shapes and a glowing lilac sky. No fire or flames.
- **Candy wave:** The person stands beside a candy-painted classic sedan riding a giant
  ocean wave, with spray in the air and a bright sky. No fire or flames.
- **Ward at night:** The person stands on a neighbourhood street of small wooden houses
  under big live oak trees, lit by purple streetlights, with a candy-painted sedan at the
  kerb. No fire or flames.
- **Front page:** The person is the photo on a newspaper front page: grey columns, smaller
  photos and empty headline space around them, on off-white newsprint. No fire or flames.
- **Behind the fence:** The person stands behind a chain-link fence in hard daylight, shot
  on grainy film. No fire or flames.

**Person and layout space:** the same as New Orleans (`core/styles.py`).

## Product notes

- **Purple prints darker on fabric** than on screen: lift the purples slightly for shirts.
- **Big letters** suits posters and hoodies best; on a phone case the name may need to be
  smaller.

---

## Avoid

| Never include | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Drug references: purple drinks in foam cups, syrup bottles | Not right for merch; purple stays a colour, not a drink |
| Car badges and grilles that identify a brand | Trademarks: the cars stay generic "classic luxury sedans" |
| Sports team logos (on jerseys or caps) | Trademarks |
| Real artists, label names or logos | Their likeness and trademarks |
| The real Parental Advisory mark | A trademark |
| Hospital and injury imagery (one cover in the research) | Not right for merch |
| Guns in hands or pointed at the viewer | Not right for merch |
| Readable newspaper text | Made-up shapes only, so nothing false is printed |
