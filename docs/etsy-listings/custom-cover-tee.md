## Filling an order

0. Check the photos and words first: [photo-checks.md](photo-checks.md).
1. Load the buyer's photos (scan screen, P), as a subject under their first name.
2. Artwork screen: their style and a scene, 4 previews, pick one, then a 2K final.
3. Lettering screen: name, title, featuring, sticker. S saves it.
4. Send a watermarked proof. After approval, X exports, and `tee.png` goes to the provider.
5. Delete the subject's photos once it ships.
