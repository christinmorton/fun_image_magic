# Listing: Custom Cover Phone Case

The cover on a phone case, in its own tall layout: the name below the camera corner, the
artwork, then the title. **List this later:** each phone model is a variation, which makes
the listing harder to keep right.

## Title

```text
Custom 90s Rap Album Cover Phone Case, Your Photo on a Southern Rap Cover Case for iPhone and Samsung, Hip Hop Gift
```

## Price and variations

- **Price:** $32.99, free shipping in the US.
- **Variation 1, Phone model:** the most common models the provider offers (for example the
  last three iPhone and Samsung Galaxy lines). Leave the rest out.
- **Variation 2, Case type:** Tough, or Slim.
- **Blank:** the provider's printed case. Each model has its own print template, so check
  that the tall layout (1500 x 3000 px) fits each one, especially around the camera.

## Personalization

Turn it on, required, 256 characters:

```text
Name on the case, title, and style (Gold & Fire, Dark & Gritty, Candy Purple or West Coast Sun). Send 3 selfies by message after ordering.
```

## Description

```text
Carry your own 90s Southern rap album cover everywhere.

Send me your selfies and I'll put you on a custom cover in the style of the classic late-90s
rap era: gold and fire, big shiny lettering, the whole bling-era look. Your name sits below
the camera in iced-out, gold or chrome letters, with your title along the bottom. Then it's
printed on a phone case for your model.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Order, choose your phone model, and add your name, title and style in the personalization box.
2. Send me 3 selfies through Etsy Messages (I'll message you what to send).
3. Within 2 business days you get a proof to approve. One round of changes is included.
4. Once you approve, your case is printed and shipped.

PICK A STYLE
- Gold & Fire: palaces, flames, cash and chandeliers
- Dark & Gritty: midnight graveyards, smoke and red light
- Candy Purple: candy-paint cars and purple night skies
- West Coast Sun: sunny streets, palm trees and lowriders

THE DETAILS
- A layout made for the case, with the camera corner kept clear
- Your name in gold, ice, chrome, stone or brush lettering
- Tough or slim case

GOOD TO KNOW
- Double-check your phone model before ordering: cases can't be swapped.
- Everyone pictured must be 18 or older and agree to be on the design. No celebrities.
- No real artist, label or brand names, please.
- The cover artwork is made with AI from your photos; the lettering, layout and finishing are
  designed by me.
- Printed and shipped by my production partner.
- Made to order, so no returns or exchanges, but I'll make it right if it arrives misprinted or damaged.
- Your photos are only used for your design and deleted once your order ships.
```
