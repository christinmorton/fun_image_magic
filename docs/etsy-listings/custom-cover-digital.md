### How to list a custom digital item

Etsy's digital listings deliver a file automatically at purchase, but this one is made after
the order. The usual way is a digital listing whose download is a short PDF ("what happens
next"), with the finished cover sent through Etsy Messages. Check Etsy's current rules on
custom digital items before listing.

**Cover Studio needs one addition for this:** a 3000 x 3000 px export of the cover (the
lettering screen saves 3600 px, so it's close). Music streaming sites ask for 3000 x 3000
JPG or PNG covers with no blur or small text. Check the distributor's current rules.
