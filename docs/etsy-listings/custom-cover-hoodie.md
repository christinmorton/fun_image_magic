1. Order, and add your name, title and style in the personalization box.
2. Send me 3 selfies through Etsy Messages (I'll message you what to send).

4. Once you approve, your hoodie is printed and shipped.

PICK A STYLE
- Gold & Fire: palaces, flames, cash and chandeliers

THE DETAILS
- Your name in gold, ice, chrome, stone or brush lettering
- Optional "featuring" list for your friends' names
- A fun parody sticker on the cover, or none
- Unisex fit with a front pocket and hood: check the size chart in the photos

GOOD TO KNOW
- Everyone pictured must be 18 or older and agree to be on the design. No celebrities.
- No real artist, label or brand names, please.
- The cover artwork is made with AI from your photos; the lettering, layout and finishing are

- Your photos are only used for your design and deleted once your order ships.

A gift they'll actually wear all winter.
