order, send photos by Etsy Messages, the design is made in Cover Studio, a proof is
approved, then it's printed. No AI is spent until an order is paid.

## Before listing anything

- **Pick the print-on-demand provider** (for example Printful or Printify) and check its
  blanks, print areas and base prices against the prices here. The prices are starting
  points, not researched.
- **Set orders to wait for you.** Provider apps usually send Etsy orders to print by
  themselves. Personalized orders must be held until the approved design is attached.
- **Make the sample gallery:** finished covers of yourself and friends who agreed, put on
  the photo mockups with the mockup tool. This is the one AI cost before any sales.
- **Check Etsy's current rules** on production partners (the print company is named in the
  listing) and on disclosing AI. The descriptions below already say both.
- **Tags** were written by hand, not from search data. Check them with real search numbers
  before launch.

## Shared by every listing

**Processing time:** proof within 2 business days of getting the photos, then 2 to 5
business days to print, plus shipping.

**Changes:** one round of changes to the proof is included (words, lettering, sticker,
framing). A different scene or style counts as the round.

**Returns:** made to order, so no returns or exchanges, except for a misprint or damage.

**Free shipping:** Etsy favours US listings with free shipping on orders of $35 or more,
so the prices here include shipping where they can.

**Who can be on a design:** adults (18+) who agreed to it. No celebrities or public figures.
No real artist, label or brand names in the words. Check every order's photos with
[photo-checks.md](photo-checks.md) before making the design.

## The photo message

Send this after every photo order (Etsy Messages takes photos):

> Thanks for your order! To make your cover I need 3 clear selfies:
>
> 1. Looking straight at the camera
> 2. Head turned a little to your left
> 3. Head turned a little to your right
>
> Good light on your face, no sunglasses or hats, just you in the picture, and your face
> filling about half the photo. One good straight-on photo works too, but three look closer.
>
> Your proof comes within 2 business days of the photos. Your photos are only used for
> your design and are deleted once your order ships.

A small picture showing the three poses, sent with the message, helps more than the words.
