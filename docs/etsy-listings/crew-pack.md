# Listing: Matching Crew Shirts

One cover with the whole crew on it, up to 6 people, printed on a shirt for each of them.
It's for birthdays, bachelor and bachelorette parties, trips, teams and friend groups.
Bigger orders: one design, several shirts.

**List this after testing a real crew.** Putting everyone in one AI request is built but
hasn't been tried on a real group yet (merch plan, step 30). Make a cover of yourself and
2 or 3 friends first and check that every face still looks like the right person.

## Title

```text
Matching Crew Shirts, Custom 90s Rap Album Cover With Your Group, Birthday Squad, Bachelor Party, Friends Trip Tees
```

## Price and variations

- **Price:** $32.99 per shirt, minimum 3 shirts. Quantity is the number of shirts. Set the
  listing's minimum quantity if Etsy allows it; otherwise say it in the title photos and
  description.
- **Variation 1, Size:** S, M, L, XL, 2XL (+$2), 3XL (+$4). Buyers with mixed sizes list
  them in the personalization box.
- **Variation 2, Colour:** Black, White, Sand, Heather Grey. The whole crew gets one colour.
- **Blank and print:** the same as the cover tee.

## Personalization

Turn it on, required, 256 characters:

```text
Crew name, title, style (Gold & Fire, Dark & Gritty, Candy Purple, West Coast Sun), and each person's name and shirt size. Up to 6 people. Each sends selfies by message.
```

## Description

```text
Put your whole crew on one 90s Southern rap album cover, then everybody gets the shirt.

Send me everyone's selfies and I'll put the crew side by side on a custom cover in the style
of the classic late-90s rap era: the crew name in iced-out, gold or chrome letters, the title
along the bottom, and everyone's names in the "featuring" list. Then a shirt is printed for
each person.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Choose the number of shirts (3 or more) and add the crew name, title, style, and each
   person's name and size in the personalization box.
2. Send me selfies of each person through Etsy Messages (I'll message you what to send).
3. Within 3 business days you get a proof to approve. One round of changes is included.
4. Once you approve, all the shirts are printed and shipped together.

PICK A STYLE
- Gold & Fire: palaces, flames, cash and chandeliers
- Dark & Gritty: midnight streets, smoke and red light
- Candy Purple: candy-paint cars and purple night skies
- West Coast Sun: sunny streets, palm trees and lowriders

THE DETAILS
- Up to 6 people on one cover
- The crew name in gold, ice, chrome, stone or brush lettering
- Everyone's names in the "featuring" list
- Order shirts for crew members who aren't on the cover too

GOOD TO KNOW
- Everyone pictured must be 18 or older and agree to be on the design. No celebrities.
- Please ask each person before sending their photos.
- No real artist, label or brand names, please.
- The cover artwork is made with AI from your photos; the lettering, layout and finishing are
  designed by me.
- Printed and shipped by my production partner.
- Made to order, so no returns or exchanges, but I'll make it right if it arrives misprinted or damaged.
- Your photos are only used for your design and deleted once your order ships.

Order at least 3 weeks before your event.
```

## Filling an order

0. Check every person's photos and the words first: [photo-checks.md](photo-checks.md).
   Make sure the buyer confirmed that everyone on the cover agreed.
1. Load each person as their own subject (scan screen, P).
2. Artwork screen: Ctrl-click every member to make a crew, pick the Crew line-up scene or
   another, then 4 previews and a final.
3. L opens the crew row layout, with the members in the featuring list. N sets the crew name.
4. Send a proof. After approval, X exports, and `tee.png` goes to the provider once per
   size ordered.
5. Delete everyone's photos once it ships.
